# International (non-Turkish) funding, grant, accelerator and credit programmes for KAAPA (state as of 9 October 2026)

Scope note: international programmes only (Turkish state/private sources are covered elsewhere). All pages were fetched on 9 Oct 2026 unless a different date is shown. "Secondary" marks aggregator or consultant sources; "headline only" means only the article title was seen. Fit judgements are in the Inferences subsections, kept apart from sourced facts. Profile used for fit: no company yet, live demo with test data, no users or revenue, solo non-developer founder, limited spoken English, Turkey first then Europe/Balkans, Claude-based AI layer planned, stack Supabase (on AWS) + Vercel + React.

## 1. EU programmes open to Turkish entities in 2026 (EIC Accelerator, EIT Culture & Creativity, Creative Europe MEDIA, Eurimages, EBRD Star Venture and similar)

### Takeaway
Türkiye is associated to Horizon Europe and is an EIT RIS country, so Horizon-family instruments (EIC Accelerator, EIT Culture & Creativity) are legally open to Turkish companies. Creative Europe is not: Türkiye left in 2017 and was "removed from the list" of 2021-2027 participants in April 2024, and the MEDIA "Innovative tools and business models" call requires establishment in, and ownership by nationals of, participating countries. Eurimages (Türkiye is still a member) funds films, not software. Before incorporation, the only EU options KAAPA's founder can use are an EIC short application as a natural person (low realistic fit) and the Erasmus for Young Entrepreneurs exchange (no company required).

### Cited Findings

**Türkiye's participation status**
- The EIT RIS page lists the "Horizon Europe Associated Countries" eligible for RIS as Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Israel, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Serbia, **Türkiye** and Ukraine. This confirms Türkiye's Horizon Europe association and RIS status — [EIT RIS](https://www.eit.europa.eu/our-activities/eit-regional-innovation-scheme)
- RIS benefits: local hubs acting as gateways to EIT services, plus a "Regional Innovation Booster" for start-ups/scale-ups in modest and moderate innovator countries (piloted in Poland in 2025, "broader pilot in at least four countries planned from 2026") — [EIT RIS](https://www.eit.europa.eu/our-activities/eit-regional-innovation-scheme)
- Creative Europe: Turkey decided to leave the programme with effect from 1 January 2017, about a year after becoming a partial member. Reported reason: a Creative Europe-funded concert project ("Aghet") on the 1915 Armenian killings. By then Creative Europe had allocated about €2.4m to Turkish projects — [Cineuropa, 10 Oct 2016](https://www.cineuropa.org/en/newsdetail/318362)
- The first official negotiation meeting on Türkiye's participation in Creative Europe 2021-2027 took place on 27 September 2021. The page does not report any conclusion of the talks — [Directorate for EU Affairs, ab.gov.tr](https://ab.gov.tr/52728_en.html)
- The official EC "list of 3rd country participation – Creative Europe" (v9.0, 15.10.2025) gives these associated countries:
  - Full participation: Iceland, Norway, Liechtenstein, Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Serbia, Ukraine (Ukraine's full MEDIA participation from 22 Sep 2025).
  - Partial participation: Georgia (Culture, Cross-sectoral and some MEDIA actions), Kosovo, Armenia and Tunisia (Culture and Desks only). Moldova expected from 1 Jan 2026 (Culture and Desks only).
  - On Türkiye: v2.0 said "Interest of Turkey confirmed"; **v7.0 (15.04.2024): "Türkiye removed from the list."**
  - Source: [EC list, hosted by Creative Europe Desk Germany](https://creative-europe-desk.de/files/inline-files/list-3rd-country-participation_crea_en_2025.pdf)
- Moldova signed its accession agreement for 2026 (headline only) — [EU Neighbours East](https://euneighbourseast.eu/news/latest-news/creative-europe-eu-and-moldova-sign-agreement-on-countrys-accession-to-programme-in-2026/)

**EIC Accelerator (Horizon Europe)**
- What it gives:
  - Grant as a lump sum "below €2.5 million" for innovation activities at TRL 6-8, completed within 24 months.
  - Investment (equity or quasi-equity) of €1-10m, with higher amounts under STEP ScaleUp. Grant and investment can be combined (blended finance).
  - Business Acceleration Services (coaches, mentors, partnering) for all EIC-supported companies.
  - Source: [EIC official](https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-accelerator_en)
  - A consultant summary gives equity of €0.5-10m and a 2026 Accelerator budget of €634m, and says the 2026 Work Programme was approved on 6 Nov 2025 — [Hezelburcht, 4 Dec 2025 (secondary)](https://www.hezelburcht.com/en/news/eic-launches-2026-work-programme-an-overview-of-the-eic-instruments-for-2026/). Note: €0.5m conflicts with the official €1m minimum.
- Who can apply:
  - Single start-ups and SMEs; small mid-caps (up to 499 employees) for investment only.
  - "Natural persons or legal entities willing to set up an SME or mid-cap."
  - Entities from EU Member States and Horizon Europe associated countries. Third-country applicants may relocate, but before the full application (Step 2).
  - The only explicit restriction is the UK, which is grant-only. The page shows no equity restriction for other associated countries.
  - Source: [EIC official](https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-accelerator_en)
- Process and dates:
  - Short application can be submitted any time: a 12-page form, a deck of up to 10 slides and a video of up to 3 minutes. Applications are batched "every first Tuesday of the month at 5pm Brussels time", reviewed by 4 remote evaluators, and feedback takes about 4-6 weeks.
  - **2026 full-application cut-offs: 7 Jan, 4 Mar, 6 May, 8 Jul, 2 Sep, 4 Nov 2026** (5pm Brussels). The next one is **4 November 2026**.
  - 2027 cut-offs were not published on the page.
  - An online technology-expert interview follows about 3-4 weeks after batching. Top proposals, up to 2.5 times the grant budget, go to an EIC Jury interview. A Seal of Excellence goes to proposals scoring at least 13/15 that are not funded.
  - Source: [EIC official](https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-accelerator_en)
- Language and interview:
  - Proposals "may be written in any EU language".
  - The jury interview is online: a 10-minute presentation, then 35 minutes of Q&A.
  - Only employed staff, board members and investors may take part. "Consultants or other third parties must not take part in the interview."
  - Evaluators expect a European or international dimension ("a purely national market won't be supported") and at least TRL 6.
  - The page does not state the interview language or whether interpreters are allowed.
  - Source: [EIC tips for applicants](https://eic.ec.europa.eu/tips-applicants-eic-accelerator_en)
- Competition:
  - 923 full applications at the 1 Oct 2025 cut-off, requesting almost €7bn; 74% asked for blended finance; applicants from 32 countries; jury pitches 19-23 Jan 2026 — [EIC news, 17 Oct 2025](https://eic.ec.europa.eu/news/strong-interest-eic-accelerator-funding-923-companies-applying-final-2025-cut-2025-10-17_en)
  - The EIC page carries a news item, "38 start-ups and SMEs secure EIC support", for the latest round (round date not captured) — [EIC official](https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-accelerator_en)
- National contact point: TÜBİTAK's Ufuk Avrupa portal runs EIC Accelerator Q&A webinars (headline only, year not verified) — [ufukavrupa.org.tr](https://ufukavrupa.org.tr/en/news/webinar-ask-anything-about-eic-accelerator-our-experts-will-be-organized-28th-august)

  - Shape needs early market validation (pilot customers or initial revenue).
  - Scale needs product-market fit, recurring revenue and at least €200k raised or under signed term sheets.
  - Source: [EIT Culture & Creativity](https://eit-culture-creativity.eu/your-opportunities/calls-funding/financial-support-startups-and-scaleups-shape-acceleration-scale)
- Topics and dates:
  - The 2027 cohort covers **Fashion, Architecture and Audio Visual Media**.
  - Call opened 10 Aug 2026; **deadline 12 Oct 2026, 17:00 CEST**. The page metadata says 10 Oct, so confirm. Results mid-Dec 2026; start Jan 2027.
  - Language and remote format are not stated. Workshops and info sessions are online.
  - Source: [EIT Culture & Creativity](https://eit-culture-creativity.eu/your-opportunities/calls-funding/financial-support-startups-and-scaleups-shape-acceleration-scale)
- Overall 2026 volume: "€8.6m in funding programmes" (headline only) — [Advanced Television, 11 Aug 2026](https://www.advanced-television.com/2026/08/11/eit-culture-creativity-opens-e8-6m-in-funding-programmes/)
- Venture Factory: a 2023 call (closed 31 May 2023) funded consortia of business-support organisations, up to €325k a year, to run incubation for cultural and creative founders. Strand 2 required a RIS-country partner. Projects could seek continuation for 2026-2027. It was not a direct grant to start-ups — [Cyprus portal](https://www.fundingprogrammesportal.gov.cy/en/call/eit-culture-and-creativity-venture-factory-call/)

**Erasmus for Young Entrepreneurs (EU Single Market Programme)**
- Türkiye is listed among the "SMP third countries". New entrepreneurs must reside in an EU or SMP country; stays last up to 6 months with an experienced host entrepreneur — [EYE official](https://www.erasmus-entrepreneurs.eu/page.php?pid=003)
- Details from a secondary source:
  - Eligible new entrepreneur: firmly planning a business with a viable plan, or less than 3 years of total entrepreneurial experience. **No registered company required.** Age 18+.
  - Monthly lump sum of €600-€1,200 depending on destination; 1-6 months within 12 months.
  - No language test, but the entrepreneur must be able to communicate with the host (about B2 suggested; English encouraged).
  - Apply personally online through a local intermediary organisation; rolling, no deadline.
  - Source: [Opportunities for Youth, 31 Aug 2026 (secondary)](https://opportunitiesforyouth.org/2026/08/31/erasmus-for-young-entrepreneurs-2026-apply-for-a-funded-1-6-month-international-business-exchange/)

### Inferences
- **Fit summary (EU block):**

| Programme | Fit for KAAPA | Why |
|---|---|---|

The founder must handle 35 minutes of Q&A himself with no consultants, and the interview language is unstated (in practice English). A production-finance SaaS will struggle to look "breakthrough". |
| EIT C&C Shape/Scale | After incorporation and first pilot customers | Turkish companies are eligible. Audiovisual is a 2027 topic, and Türkiye's RIS status lifts Scale to €500k. The current call (deadline 12 Oct 2026) needs a registered company and pilot customers or revenue, so KAAPA cannot apply now. Watch for the next call (by analogy, about Aug-Oct 2027; topics rotate, unverified). |

The ownership clause means even a KAAPA subsidiary in, say, Serbia or North Macedonia would likely fail if owned by a Turkish national. Possible only as a non-beneficiary role, if the call allows it (unverified). |
| Eurimages | Not relevant (funding) | Films only, not software. |
| EBRD Star Venture | After incorporation, when a Türkiye call opens | Free advisory and investor network; no cash. |
| Erasmus for Young Entrepreneurs | **Now** (with a caveat) | No company needed, rolling, funded stay with a host entrepreneur (e.g. a European production-software company). Caveat: the founder's 20+ years in production may count as "entrepreneurial experience" if he ran his own company, which could exceed the under-3-years limit. Host communication needs about B2. |

- Turkish is not an official EU language, so "any EU language" for EIC proposals in practice means writing in English (or another EU language).
- EIC timing: a short application submitted now would be batched on 3 Nov 2026 (the first Tuesday of November), with feedback around December 2026. That makes the 4 Nov 2026 cut-off unrealistic; the earliest full application would fall at a 2027 cut-off, whose dates are not yet published.
- Success odds: the official sources give only counts (923 full applications at the Oct 2025 cut-off; "38" funded in a recent round). Funded-to-full-application ratios are probably in the low single-digit percentages, but this pairing is not verified.

- EIC 2027 cut-off dates are not published yet (Work Programme 2027 pending).

## 2. Cloud, AI and infrastructure credit programmes (2026)

### Takeaway
Self-serve tiers without an investor are small: AWS $1k (up to $5k), Google $2k, Microsoft $1k plus $4k after business verification, Supabase $300. The large tiers (AWS up to $200k, Google $200-350k, Microsoft $100-150k, Vercel up to $30k, Anthropic VC-partner up to $100k) need an investor or accelerator referral, or funding. For Claude specifically:
- Anthropic's own startup credits apply to the first-party API only and are currently "over capacity".
- AWS credits were announced in 2024 as usable for Claude on Bedrock; this needs re-confirming.
- Google's credits explicitly exclude third-party models such as Claude.

### Cited Findings

**AWS Activate**
- Tiers:
  - Founders (self-funded): "Up to $5,000 USD in Activate Credits", starting at $1,000; select participants may get more.
  - Portfolio: "Up to $200,000 USD", requiring an Organization ID from an Activate Provider (accelerator, angel or VC).
  - AWS Credits for AI Startups: "$200,000+", invite-only, for startups past Portfolio.
  - Source: [AWS Activate credits](https://aws.amazon.com/activate/credits)
- Eligibility: "Pre-series B, founded in the last 10 years, AWS Account on Paid Tier Plan" and "new to Activate Credits or requesting more credits than previously received". Credits can support products built on Amazon Bedrock. The page does not mention Anthropic or third-party models — [AWS Activate credits](https://aws.amazon.com/activate/credits)
- Promotional credit terms:
  - Credits are not applied to "AWS Marketplace, AWS Professional Services, AWS Training, AWS Certification" unless AWS authorises it, nor to Route 53 domain registration or upfront fees (Savings Plans or Reserved Instances).
  - Each code can be redeemed once, on one account. Credits are void if the account was created solely to obtain them, and they expire on the date given with the code.
  - Bedrock third-party models are not mentioned.
  - Source: [AWS Promotional Credit Terms](https://aws.amazon.com/awscredits/)
- In April 2024 AWS said credit recipients can apply AWS credits to third-party models on Amazon Bedrock, including Anthropic's Claude; YC startups got $500k in Bedrock credits — [TechRadar, 3 Apr 2024](https://www.techradar.com/pro/amazon-offers-free-credits-for-startups-to-use-ai-models-including-anthropic)
- Supabase perk listed on AWS Startups: "$300 in Supabase credits, distributed over 12 months". Eligibility not stated — [AWS Startups offer: Supabase](https://aws.amazon.com/startups/offers/supabase)

**Google for Startups Cloud Program**
- Tiers: Pre-funded $2,000 "to build an MVP"; Early Stage (seed to Series A) $200,000, or up to $350,000 for AI-first startups; Series B+ customised — [Google for Startups Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/startup)
- AI tier:
  - Year 1 covers 100% of spend up to $100k (up to $250k if AI-first); Year 2 covers 20% up to another $100k.
  - Requires VC/institutional equity (pre-seed or seed within 5 years, or Series A in the last 12 months), founded within 5 years, and no more than $5k of prior Google Cloud credits. The core product must use Gemini.
  - Angel, friends-and-family, grant, prize and crowdfunding money does not qualify for this tier.
  - **"Third-party models are billed directly and are not covered by program credits."**
  - Source: [Google for Startups – AI](https://cloud.google.com/startup/ai)

**Microsoft for Startups**
- The official page offers "up to $150,000 in credits" usable "across eligible Azure services". No tier split, eligibility or Claude/Foundry information on the page — [Microsoft for Startups](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/startups)
- Detail from a secondary guide (published 24 Aug 2026, reviewed 7 Oct 2026):
  - $200 on creating an Azure account.
  - No-investor offer: $1,000 for 90 days, plus $4,000 for 180 days after business verification.
  - Investor-referral offer (code from an Investor Network partner): usually $100,000 to start, up to $150,000.
  - Criteria: B2B software/AI/tech startup at or working toward MVP, privately held, for-profit, with no more than $350,000 in lifetime free Azure credits.
  - "Founders Hub" branding has gone, with no shutdown notice published.
  - Coverage of Claude in Azure AI Foundry is not addressed.
  - Source: [Causo guide (secondary)](https://hub.causo.ai/guides/how-to-apply-to-microsoft-for-startups-2026)

**Vercel for Startups**
- Up to "$30,000 in Flexible Commitment Amount", plus Enterprise-tier access and startup support.
- Apply through a form asking for name, work email, company size, company website and country. Eligibility criteria are not published; the page shows VC logos (Founders Fund, Greylock, Index).
- Source: [Vercel for Startups](https://vercel.com/startups)

**Anthropic (status line only, as instructed)**
- The Claude for Startups page says: "we're currently over capacity on the Claude Team and $1,000 API credit offers", and that Anthropic is "reshaping" the programme.
- Remaining: partner "Claude Startup Stack" offers worth up to $45,000, 45-minute Applied AI office hours every two weeks (live, English), and events.

- Source: [Claude for Startups](https://claude.com/programs/startups)

## 3. Media/entertainment-tech and B2B SaaS accelerators, and festival/market tech labs

### Takeaway
The most precisely targeted international programme is **EFM Startups (Berlinale)**: its 2026 edition focused on "tools for producers". It requires an English-language website, a 3-minute English stage pitch, four online trainings and founder presence in Berlin, and it gives visibility, a market badge and investor/industry meetings, not cash. Cannes Next (Marché du Film) offers start-up exhibition space and co-created a CES Innovation Award category explicitly covering "tools for producers … and financiers". The European media-tech accelerator found (MediaTech Hub Potsdam) closed its 2026 intake on 30 Jun 2026. No start-up track was found at Series Mania; MIPCOM was not researched.

### Cited Findings

## 4. Global generalist accelerators realistic for a solo, non-English-speaking founder

### Takeaway
Mainstream global accelerators run in English and in person:
- **YC** "regularly" accepts solo founders and applicants from anywhere. It expects the team to be able to build the product itself (usually meaning a technical co-founder) and requires a US/Canada/Singapore/Cayman parent company.
- **Antler** is built for solo or pre-idea founders but requires 100% full-time, mostly in-person residency.
- **Techstars** invests $220k through rolling programmes.
- **Founder Institute** advertises a Turkey 2026 programme for idea and pre-seed founders and is the most accessible of the four, though its language and fees were not verified.

### Cited Findings
**Y Combinator**
- Invests "$500,000 in each company" — [YC FAQ](https://www.ycombinator.com/faq). Structure: $125K post-money SAFE (7%) plus $375K uncapped MFN; acceptance under 1% (Summer 2025 about 0.6%) — [Causo H1 2026 accelerator report (secondary)](https://hub.causo.ai/guides/h1-2026-accelerator-report)
- Policies:
  - "We regularly accept solo founders"; open "to any startup, anywhere in the world"; no incorporation needed before applying.
  - Companies incorporated outside the US, Canada, Singapore or the Cayman Islands must create a parent company there.
  - "It's important for the founding team to have the skills to build their product themselves", which "usually means you need a technical co-founder".
  - The batch is in person in San Francisco, starting with a 3-day retreat.
  - Source: [YC FAQ](https://www.ycombinator.com/faq)
- **Winter 2027 (Jan-Mar, SF): on-time deadline 2 Nov 2026, 8pm PT**; decisions by 11 Dec. Interviews are mostly by video in Nov-Dec. Early Decision is open for later batches — [YC Apply](https://www.ycombinator.com/apply)

**Techstars**
- $220K: a $200K uncapped MFN SAFE plus a $20K post-money CEA (5% common floor plus MFN). 13 weeks; 50+ programmes in 30+ cities; rolling, per city; acceptance under 1% on most tracks — [Causo H1 2026 report (secondary)](https://hub.causo.ai/guides/h1-2026-accelerator-report)

**Antler**
- Solo founders are "a core part of the thesis", with structured co-founder matching. Pre-idea founders are accepted. Requires 100% full-time commitment; "most locations require physical presence".
- Terms: UK £125k for 8.5%, plus an £85k MFN convertible and follow-on capital up to £500k in total.
- 2026 deadlines: Germany 14 Sep, UK 22 Sep (both passed); US rolling.
- Process: free online form, first-round screen, 2-3 partner interviews; residency of 10-12 weeks.
- Source: [Causo Antler 2026 guide (secondary)](https://hub.causo.ai/guides/how-to-apply-to-antler-2026)

**Founder Institute (US-based, with a Turkey chapter)**
- Advertises the "Turkey 2026" programme for idea and pre-seed founders, with free online events from October to December 2026. Format, language, fees and equity are not stated on the page (article dated 15 Jun 2025) — [Founder Institute Turkey](https://fi.co/insight/build-a-great-startup-in-2025-with-the-fi-turkey-startup-accelerator)
- "Founder Institute Turkey 2025 produces 4 new technology companies" (headline only) — [Founder Institute](https://fi.co/insight/founder-institute-turkey-2025-produces-4-new-technology-companies)
