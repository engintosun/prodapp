# Turkish private funding sources for a pre-seed B2B vertical SaaS (KAAPA), state as of 9 October 2026

Scope: Turkey-based private investors and programs only. State grants are excluded, except where a private or semi-private actor is also a TÜBİTAK BiGG implementing organization (uygulayıcı kuruluş, UK). Researched on 9 Oct 2026. Some primary sites blocked automated reading (workup.com.tr, mevzuat.gov.tr) or returned empty pages (startershub.com), so a few items rest on secondary sources. Those items are flagged.

## 1. Which Turkish pre-seed and seed VC funds were actively investing in 2025-2026 in B2B/vertical SaaS, fintech, creative/media tech or AI applications? Typical tickets and requirements

### Takeaway
AI (48 deals) and SaaS (40 deals) were the two most active sectors by deal count in Turkey in 2025, and 269 of the 360 deals were seed-stage. A vertical SaaS with an AI layer therefore sits in the categories with the most deals. However, the institutional funds that publish ticket sizes write cheques far above pre-seed: Collective Spark's first cheque is USD 500k-1M, and TechOne writes up to USD 1.5M. In practice the 2025 pre-seed layer was filled by TÜBİTAK BiGG Fund tickets (about USD 21-26k), crowdfunding platforms, small GSYF tickets and angels. Every channel other than crowdfunding assumes an incorporated company.

### Cited Findings
Market level (2025):
- Turkish startup investment was about USD 1.4B across 360 deals in 2025, down from USD 2.6B but up from 331 deals in 2024. The drop is attributed to the absence of mega-deals. Acquisitions made up 64.3% of volume and early stage 27.7%. Seed deals numbered 269, about 75% of the deal count. — [Fintechtime, 3 Mar 2026, on the KPMG Türkiye and 212 report](https://fintechtime.com/2026/03/turkiyedeki-startup-yatirimlari-2025-yilinda-14-milyar-abd-dolari-seviyesinde-gerceklesti/)
- Domestic investors took part in 318 of the 360 deals and foreign investors in 42. Foreign money was about USD 1.145B (about 82% of volume), against about USD 227M local. — [Fintechtime](https://fintechtime.com/2026/03/turkiyedeki-startup-yatirimlari-2025-yilinda-14-milyar-abd-dolari-seviyesinde-gerceklesti/)

### Inferences
- Published first cheques from institutional VCs (USD 500k+) are aimed at companies with traction. They are out of reach for a pre-revenue, pre-company project. In 2025 the realistic first money in Turkey came from four sources:
  - BiGG-size tickets of about USD 21-26k.
  - Platform or angel rounds of USD 100-200k (Dikont, Abonesepeti).
  - Small GSYF tickets of about USD 300k (Webrazzi GSYF, see section 4).
  - Studio or co-investment players (Fark Labs at USD 600k with Cevher Group).
- Bank and corporate GSYFs (Yapı Kredi FRWRD, FIBA Fırsat, Maxis, TT Ventures GSYF) show up as syndicates in multi-million rounds such as OctoXLabs. They are a seed-plus or Series A route, not pre-seed.
- Every equity investor in these sources (VC, GSYF, angel) needs a legal entity to take shares in. Crowdfunding is the only exception (see section 5), so no fund is realistic for KAAPA before it becomes an anonim şirket. This is inferred from the requirement that shares be issued; no fund explicitly states it.
- A Claude-based AI layer plus SaaS lines up with the two highest deal-count categories of 2025. That is a positioning point, not a guarantee.

### Gaps
- Pre-seed is not separated from seed in the KPMG/212 data. I found no pre-seed-specific statistics for 2025-2026.
- Startups.watch's 2025 "most active investors" rankings were not reached (likely paywalled). Investor deal counts are unavailable.
- Ticket sizes and explicit requirements for Revo, Boğaziçi Ventures, APY, Simya, Kayacan, Inveo, Diffusion and Ludus are not published in the sources read. Each fund's own site would need checking.
- Sizes and closing dates of the "new" 2025-2026 funds (212 Fund III, Revo Fund III, Simya II, etc.) were not given.
- I found no Turkish fund with a stated mandate for creative-industry B2B software or film/TV production tools.

## 2. Licensed angel investor networks (BKS) — how founders apply, what they invest in, and the angel tax incentive

### Takeaway
The licensing authority is still the Hazine ve Maliye Bakanlığı, not the Sanayi ve Teknoloji Bakanlığı. A new BKS regulation (Resmî Gazete 31.12.2024, effective 1 Jan 2025) loosened network, licensing and investment rules, and amounts are indexed yearly (YDO 25.49% for 2026). The investee must be a full-taxpayer anonim şirket no more than 10 years old. Licensed angels deduct 75% of the investment, or 100% if the project had TÜBİTAK, KOSGEB or Sanayi Bakanlığı program support in the prior 5 years. They must hold for 2 years, and the minimum investment for tax support is 250,980 TL in 2026. Founders apply to networks, not to the ministry. Formally registered BKS deal flow is small: 98 investments and 65.7M TL cumulative as of Q1 2025.

### Cited Findings
Regulation and incentive:
- New regulation published in RG 31.12.2024 (No. 32769), effective 1 Jan 2025.
- Ministry pre-approval of investments was removed; approval now happens on final documents.
- The 1,000,000 TL per-investment cap and the 20-company cap were removed.
- The application window after capital transfer is 6 months.
- Monetary amounts are revalued yearly by the YDO (25.49% for 2026).
- Networks may operate virtually, must report every six months and must offer mentoring.
- Investments are registered on the BKS Portal (hmb.gov.tr/bks-bilgi-sistemi).
- Source for all points above: [Hazine ve Maliye Bakanlığı, "Yönetmelik ile Getirilen Yenilikler" presentation, 11.05.2026 (PDF)](https://ms.hmb.gov.tr/uploads/2026/05/1_Yonetmelik-ile-Getirilen-Yenilikler-Sunumu-11.05.2026-f92bebde5c14c8b3.pdf)
- Investee requirements: full-taxpayer anonim şirket (limited şirket is not mentioned). Prior-year net sales limit raised from 5,000,000 TL to the KOBİ small-enterprise threshold of 100,000,000 TL. No more than 10 years since establishment. Minimum investment for tax support 250,980 TL in 2026 (previously 200,000 TL). — [HMB presentation, 11.05.2026](https://ms.hmb.gov.tr/uploads/2026/05/1_Yonetmelik-ile-Getirilen-Yenilikler-Sunumu-11.05.2026-f92bebde5c14c8b3.pdf)
- Angel license thresholds for 2026: gross income 2,509,800 TL, or wealth 12,549,000 TL, or the experience criterion (employer annual turnover of at least 100,000,000 TL). — [HMB presentation, 11.05.2026](https://ms.hmb.gov.tr/uploads/2026/05/1_Yonetmelik-ile-Getirilen-Yenilikler-Sunumu-11.05.2026-f92bebde5c14c8b3.pdf)
- Deduction terms:
  - 75% of the share price is deductible from declared income.
  - 100% if the investee's project was supported in the last five years under Sanayi ve Teknoloji Bakanlığı, TÜBİTAK or KOSGEB programs.
  - Annual cap 2.5 million TL; the article does not say which year the figure applies to.
  - Unused amounts carry forward, revalued.
  - Shares must be held for at least two full years (730 days). Early sale triggers the unpaid tax plus a penalty and late interest.
  - Licenses last five years.
  - Source: [EY Türkiye, dated 12 Jun 2025](https://www.ey.com/tr_tr/insights/tax/melek-yatirimci-olmanin-sartlari-ve-vergi-avantajlari)

Network landscape and deal flow:
- As of 31 Mar 2025 there were 18 active accredited BKY networks: 10 commitment and 8 non-commitment. Locations: 10 Istanbul, 2 Antalya, and one each in Diyarbakır, İzmir, Kayseri, Ankara, Elazığ and Bursa. — [HMB, BKS İlerleme Raporu 2025 Q1 (PDF)](https://ms.hmb.gov.tr/uploads/2025/04/BKS-ILERLEME-RAPORU-1.-CEYREK-d6b0c485e0a5d980.pdf)
- Networks visible in the HMB investment chart: Trangels, Melek Ağı İstanbul, Melek Yatırımcı Merkezi, Keiretsu Forum İstanbul, Şirket Ortağım, and a truncated "Eğt. Dan. Hzm. A.Ş.". — [HMB Q1 2025 report](https://ms.hmb.gov.tr/uploads/2025/04/BKS-ILERLEME-RAPORU-1.-CEYREK-d6b0c485e0a5d980.pdf)
- 1,143 licenses have been issued since 2013 (excluding cancellations), of which 625 are active. Cumulative BKS-registered investments: 98, by 264 angels, totalling 65,690,856 TL (about 670k TL average, my calculation). Q1 2025 alone: 17 investments, 27 angels, 20,248,063 TL. Investee locations: 87 of 98 in İstanbul. — [HMB Q1 2025 report](https://ms.hmb.gov.tr/uploads/2025/04/BKS-ILERLEME-RAPORU-1.-CEYREK-d6b0c485e0a5d980.pdf)
- The Ministry links an updated accredited-network list dated 08.10.2025. I did not retrieve it. — [HMB presentation, 11.05.2026](https://ms.hmb.gov.tr/uploads/2026/05/1_Yonetmelik-ile-Getirilen-Yenilikler-Sunumu-11.05.2026-f92bebde5c14c8b3.pdf)

Galata Business Angels (GBA):
- Startups apply online (Gust or a Google Form).
- Steps: application, then pre-screening (pitch deck and a meeting with the GBA team), then a first mentor meeting with a relevant investor, then "Venture Academy", where GBA investors hear selected startups.
- GBA invests in early-stage tech with a "clear value proposition" and some traction (a set number of users or customers).
- Criteria: growth potential, scalability, team quality, exit potential, innovativeness, competitive advantage, market size.
- GBA describes itself as a non-profit association. Its site states no ticket sizes or startup fees.
- Source: [GBA site](https://galatabusinessangels.com/en)
- GBA was accredited as a BKY network in Dec 2013 (2013 data; current status not rechecked). — [Webrazzi, 13 Dec 2013](https://webrazzi.com/2013/12/13/galata-is-meleklerine-bireysel-katilim-yatirimcisi-agi/)

Other networks found (pages not read, existence only):
- Antalya Teknokent BKY Ağı — [Antalya Teknokent](https://www.antalyateknokent.com.tr/tr/antalya-teknokent-bky-agi)
- Bahariye Melek Yatırım Ağı (GİV) — [GİV](https://www.giv.org.tr/melek-yatirimcilik)
- TRAngels, Keiretsu Forum Türkiye, Arya (women-founder focus, network plus fund), StartupCentrum Angel Network — [Girisimin, 6 May 2026](https://girisimin.com/2026/05/06/turkiye-yatirim-fonlari-ve-yatirimlari-genis-liste-2026)

### Inferences
- **Order of steps matters.** BKS requires an anonim şirket, so KAAPA cannot take BKS-incentivized angel money before incorporating as an A.Ş., which is the planned company type anyway.
- **TÜBİTAK support raises the angel deduction.** If KAAPA gets BiGG (a TÜBİTAK program) first, licensed angels investing within the following 5 years can deduct 100% instead of 75%. That is a concrete selling point in an angel pitch.
- **Registered angel flow is thin.** About 98 registered investments since the system began, against 625 active licenses, so many Turkish angel deals likely happen outside the BKS tax channel. An angel's license status should not be treated as a filter.
- **GBA is not a fit yet.** Its traction requirement (users or customers) means a pilot with at least one production company is the practical entry ticket.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve the full accredited network list (HMB list dated 08.10.2025), so network names are partial.
- No network in the sources publishes typical ticket sizes. The only ministry-level figure is the about 670k TL average per registered investment as of Q1 2025.
- Not confirmed whether the 2.5M TL deduction cap is indexed for 2026.
- Pitch language is not stated by any network; presumably Turkish, but unverified.
- Keiretsu Forum's startup presenting fees in Turkey were not verified.

## 3. Accelerators and incubators open to pre-company or early teams in 2026-2027, including private BiGG implementing organizations

### Takeaway
Corporate programs run in Turkish are plentiful, but most require incorporation, an MVP and sometimes operating history: Garanti BBVA Partners asks for 2+ years, and Techworks and THY Terminal want a company or a product ready for a pilot. The doorway for a team without a company is mainly the university and technopark incubators that act as BiGG implementing organizations, such as İTÜ Çekirdek. There, TÜBİTAK's BiGG Fonu now takes equity (3% for 1,350,000 TL in the 2026-2 call), and the implementing organization or its GSYF may co-invest. Workup (deadline 31 Mar 2026) and Türk Telekom PİLOT (applications Feb to May 2026) run yearly windows in the first half of the year. Both 2026 windows are closed; the next are expected in early 2027 (inferred from the pattern).

### Cited Findings
**TÜBİTAK BiGG Yatırım: equity mechanics and the role of implementing organizations.** The grant itself is covered by another researcher; only the private co-investment angle is noted here.
- Startups first complete Stage 1 (acceleration) at an implementing organization. Only those who qualify for Stage 2 apply to the call.
- Startups awarded the "Mükemmeliyet Mührü" receive 1,350,000 TL from the TÜBİTAK BiGG Fonu for 3% equity.
- Implementing organizations can request follow-on investment of up to 1,350,000 TL during monitoring.
- The implementing organization, or entities acting for it under an agreement, may co-invest alongside BiGG Fonu. Startups with a declared co-investor get a faster track.
- 2026-2 call dates: applications 31 Aug to 30 Sep 2026; results 24-27 Nov 2026; company setup and contract by 31 Dec 2026; support starts 1 Jan 2027.
- Future calls follow a "2026-2028 Tohum Öncesi Yatırım Planı", and accelerator programs run until 31 Jul 2028.
- Source: [TÜBİTAK, BiGG Yatırım 2026-2 call page](https://tubitak.gov.tr/tr/destekler/destek/sanayi/ulusal-destek-programlari/cagri-bigg-yatirim-programi-2026-2-cagrisi-acildi)
- **Conflicting amounts:** İTÜ Çekirdek's 6 Mar 2026 announcement cites "900.000 TL yatırım desteği" after company founding, against 1,350,000 TL on the TÜBİTAK 2026-2 page. The likely cause is a different call or revaluation (unverified). — [İTÜ Çekirdek, 6 Mar 2026](https://itucekirdek.com/haberler/is-fikrinizi-hayata-gecirin-2026-itu-cekirdek-bigg-yatirim-basvurulari-basladi/); [TÜBİTAK](https://tubitak.gov.tr/tr/destekler/destek/sanayi/ulusal-destek-programlari/cagri-bigg-yatirim-programi-2026-2-cagrisi-acildi)

  - BİGG Assist: İzmir Teknoloji Geliştirme Bölgesi A.Ş., **İnci Holding A.Ş.** (a private holding, which is notable as a private actor inside an implementing consortium) and İzmir Ekonomi Üniversitesi.

### Inferences
- **Realistic in the next 12 months while staying company-less:** a BiGG implementing organization's Stage 1 program (İTÜ Çekirdek or another organization in the 2026-2028 plan). The equity is taken by the state fund (3%), but the implementing organization or its GSYF may co-invest. That makes it the main private-capital touchpoint before incorporation.
- **Workup 15th term:** likely open around Feb-Mar 2027 if the pattern holds. It is Turkish and online, and no equity is stated, so it is the most language-friendly corporate option. Whether it accepts teams without a company is unknown.
- **PİLOT 15th term:** likely Feb-May 2027, from the same pattern.
- **Out of scope for now:** Garanti BBVA Partners (needs 2+ years of operation), Techworks (needs a company) and THY Terminal (wrong sector).
- **Language:** every program found runs its application in Turkish. PİLOT's Stanford and US track is the main place English would matter, and only after selection.
- **Pitch implication:** Workup now uses AI agents to pre-screen applications. A clearly written Turkish application with concrete product evidence (the live demo of the budget module and the receipt-approval loop) matters.

## 5. Equity crowdfunding (paya dayalı kitle fonlaması) in 2026

### Takeaway
Crowdfunding is the only private equity channel in Turkey where a campaign can legally start before a company exists. The anonim şirket must be founded within 90 days after a successful campaign, and a limited company must first convert to an A.Ş. Key numbers:
- 2026 cap: 142.5M TL per 12 months, plus up to 20% extra within the cap.
- 19 active platforms and about 44,700 investors (Oct 2025).
- Market volume about 1.24B TL in 2025 (MKK data; whether this is annual or cumulative is unclear).
- Campaigns last at most 60 days. For targets above 1M TL, at least 5% must come from qualified investors.
- Obligations: a feasibility report reviewed by the platform's committee, fund-use reports every 6 months, a special-purpose audit of fund use, and 5 years of updates.
- Small SaaS rounds of USD 100-200k were completed through platforms in 2025.

### Cited Findings
- **2026 cap:** 142,500,000 TL per 12 months (previously 112,500,000 TL), under SPK Bulletin 2025-68 revaluation. Up to 20% additional funding may be raised within the cap. This comes from platform news, not the SPK text. — [Trinkfon, 6 Jan 2026](https://trinkfon.com/haberler/2026-yili-kitle-fonlamasi-limitleri-artirildi/)
- The cap had risen from 75M to 112.5M TL at the start of 2025 (headline only). — [egirişim, 8 Jan 2025](https://egirisim.com/2025/01/08/paya-dayali-kitle-fonlamada-fon-talebi-75-milyon-tlden-112-5-milyon-tlye-yukseltildi/)
- **Market size:** MKK data cited by an SPK official give 1.13B TL at end-2024 and 1.24B TL in 2025 ("2025'te bu rakam 1.24 milyar liraya yükseldi"). There are about 44,700 investors and "aktif olarak 19 platform". Halk Yatırım's Fonlabüyüsün ran nine campaigns with over 65M TL of demand. — [Para Dergi, 7/9 Oct 2025](https://www.paradergi.com.tr/girisimcilik/2025/10/09/yatirimci-sayisi-44-bini-asti)
- **Single-platform example, FonAngels (Girişim Kitle Fonlama Platformu A.Ş.), H2 2025:**
  - 6 campaigns closed, all 6 successful.
  - 180,620,719 TL raised against 164,950,000 TL requested (the table sums to 178.2M TL).
  - Median raise about 7.05M TL (my calculation from the table).
  - 2,848 investor participations.
  - Sectors: software, medical informatics, AI, fintech, biotech, health, automotive.
  - Durations: 59 days (five campaigns) and 37 days (one).
  - Source: [FonAngels H2 2025 period report (PDF)](https://fonangels.com/raporlar/2025-ikinci-donemsel-kampanya-raporu.pdf)
- **Platforms as seed investors in SaaS:** FonAngels is listed as the investor in Dikont (SaaS, USD 112,679, Aug 2025) and FonOrion in Abonesepeti (SaaS, USD 200,000, Dec 2025). The review labels some investors as crowdfunding platforms ("fonbulucu"). — [KPMG/212 review](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/tr/pdf/2026/03/turkish-startup-investments-review-2025.pdf)
- **Rules under SPK Kitle Fonlaması Tebliği III-35/A.2 (published 27.10.2021).** Source for all: [PRM Finans summary of the Tebliğ](https://www.prmfinans.com/iii-35-a-2-kitle-fonlamasi); the official mevzuat.gov.tr text blocked automated reading.
  - A campaign can run before incorporation. The A.Ş. must be founded within 90 days of the campaign's end (Art. 17(7)(a)). A limited company must convert to an A.Ş. first (Art. 16(1)). Existing shares cannot be sold (Art. 16(1)).
  - Non-qualified investor limit: 50,000 TL per calendar year, or 10% of declared annual net income capped at 200,000 TL (Art. 15(1)). These are the base figures; the current indexed values were not found.
  - A campaign lasts at most 60 days (Art. 17(2)). Funds sit in escrow, then move to the company's blocked account, and the capital increase must follow within 30 business days (Art. 17).
  - A fund-use report is required at launch and every six months (Art. 21). A special-purpose independent audit of fund use runs annually if more than 1M TL is raised (Art. 21(4)). Updates must be posted on the campaign page for 5 years (Art. 11(3)).
  - Equity campaigns need a feasibility report reviewed by the platform's investment committee (Art. 9(8)).
  - For targets above 1M TL, at least 5% must come from qualified investors (Art. 16(5)). Up to 20% extra may be raised (Art. 16(4)).
  - Platforms must publish their fees and commissions, but the Tebliğ sets no rates (Art. 11(3)(f)).
  - Debt-based crowdfunding also exists. Debt is capped at 50% of discounted future cash flows in a credibility report, the per-investor cap is 20,000 TL per project, and repayments are amortizing (Art. 18-19).

### Inferences
- **Timing against BiGG (important for KAAPA).** A successful campaign forces an A.Ş. within 90 days, which would make the founder a company partner. That would break BiGG eligibility (no company partnership at application), so crowdfunding should come after BiGG or after deciding against BiGG. This follows from the Tebliğ plus the BiGG constraint in the brief.
- **Fit.** Crowdfunding is the only route here that lets a pre-revenue, pre-company project raise publicly. It still requires a feasibility report, a valuation set in the information form, ongoing reporting and audit costs, and the 5% qualified-investor rule for targets above 1M TL, which in practice means landing at least one qualified investor or angel before launch.
- **Market reading.** 1.13B TL to 1.24B TL is more plausibly two annual figures (2024 full year, 2025 year to date) than a cumulative stock: one platform alone raised 180M TL in H2 2025 across 19 platforms. This is unresolved.
- **Typical round size.** Platform rounds in late 2025 typically ran single-digit to tens of millions of TL (FonAngels median about 7M TL), well below the 142.5M TL cap.

### Gaps
- 2026 indexed limits for non-qualified investors were not found; only the Tebliğ's base figures (50k / 200k TL) are known.
- No source gave platform commission rates, total campaign costs (feasibility report, audit, legal), or typical pre-money valuations.
- Share of pre-revenue or prototype-stage campaigns and their success rate: no data found.
- I did not retrieve the SPK's official list of licensed platforms; only "19 active" from an SPK official. Platforms seen in sources: FonAngels, FonOrion (inferred from KPMG as a platform), Fonbulucu, Fonlabüyüsün (Halk Yatırım), Fongogo, Trinkfon, Startupfon. The Bloomberg HT headline "Kitle fonlaması 1,5 milyar TL'ye dayandı" is undated and was not read.
- How a "to-be-established A.Ş." campaign interacts with BiGG eligibility should be confirmed against the 1812 call text (owned by the grants researcher).

### Gaps

## 7. Synthesis: what Turkish pre-seed investors require, and where a solo, non-technical, pre-revenue, pre-company founder stands

### Takeaway
Outside BiGG and crowdfunding, Turkish private pre-seed money expects three things: an incorporated anonim şirket, a working product with early users or customers, and a strong team. KAAPA has a working live demo and deep domain expertise. It has no company (by design), no users or pilot, no revenue and no co-founder, so most VC, angel and corporate programs are not yet accessible. The realistic order is:
1. A BiGG implementing organization's Stage 1, while still company-less.
2. Incorporate as an A.Ş.
3. One or more pilot production companies.
4. Licensed angels (with the 100% deduction after TÜBİTAK support) and/or a crowdfunding campaign.
5. Seed funds and GSYFs.

### Cited Findings
- **Entity:**
  - BKS-incentivized angel investment requires a full-taxpayer anonim şirket no more than 10 years old. — [HMB, 11.05.2026](https://ms.hmb.gov.tr/uploads/2026/05/1_Yonetmelik-ile-Getirilen-Yenilikler-Sunumu-11.05.2026-f92bebde5c14c8b3.pdf)
  - Techworks requires incorporation. — [Webrazzi](https://webrazzi.com/2026/07/28/2026-yili-techworks-hizlandirma-programi-basvurulari-basladi/)
  - BiGG 2026-2 winners must set up a company by 31 Dec 2026, and the BiGG Fonu takes 3%. — [TÜBİTAK](https://tubitak.gov.tr/tr/destekler/destek/sanayi/ulusal-destek-programlari/cagri-bigg-yatirim-programi-2026-2-cagrisi-acildi)
  - Crowdfunding allows the A.Ş. to be formed within 90 days after a successful campaign. — [PRM Finans](https://www.prmfinans.com/iii-35-a-2-kitle-fonlamasi)
- **Traction and product:**
  - GBA wants early-stage tech with "some traction" (users or customers). — [GBA](https://galatabusinessangels.com/en)
  - Garanti BBVA Partners wants an MVP, 2+ years of operation and a revenue model. — [Garanti BBVA Partners](https://www.garantibbvapartners.com)
  - Techworks wants an MVP. — [Webrazzi](https://webrazzi.com/2026/07/28/2026-yili-techworks-hizlandirma-programi-basvurulari-basladi/)
  - THY Terminal wants products mature enough for pilots. — [egirişim](https://egirisim.com/2026/05/15/girisim-hizlandirma-programi-turk-hava-yollari-terminalin-2026-yili-basvurulari-acildi/)
- **Team:**
  - GBA lists "team quality" among its criteria. — [GBA](https://galatabusinessangels.com/en)
  - Entertech GSYF wants "strong founding teams" and export ambition. — [Para Dergi](https://www.paradergi.com.tr/girisimcilik/2024/12/24/entertech-istanbul-teknokentten-400-milyon-tllik-yatirim-fonu)
- **Ticket reality in 2025:**
  - BiGG tickets were about USD 21-26k, and SaaS seed rounds through platforms and angels USD 100-200k. — [KPMG/212](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/tr/pdf/2026/03/turkish-startup-investments-review-2025.pdf)
  - Collective Spark's first cheque is USD 500k-1M. — [Girisimin](https://girisimin.com/2026/05/06/turkiye-yatirim-fonlari-ve-yatirimlari-genis-liste-2026)
  - The average BKS-registered angel investment is about 670k TL (my calculation from the Q1 2025 cumulative figures). — [HMB Q1 2025](https://ms.hmb.gov.tr/uploads/2025/04/BKS-ILERLEME-RAPORU-1.-CEYREK-d6b0c485e0a5d980.pdf)
- **Sector tailwind:** AI and SaaS were the top two sectors by 2025 deal count (48 and 40). — [KPMG/212](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/tr/pdf/2026/03/turkish-startup-investments-review-2025.pdf)

### Inferences
Where KAAPA stands against each requirement:

| Requirement | Typical Turkish expectation | KAAPA today | Gap severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Legal entity | A.Ş. (BKS, VC, most programs) | None, deliberately, for BiGG | Blocking for equity, except crowdfunding and BiGG Stage 1; resolved by incorporating after BiGG |
| Product | MVP or working product | Live demo with test data (budget module and receipt-approval loop) | Partly met; strongest asset to show |
| Traction | Users or customers (GBA); revenue model; 2+ years of operation (Garanti BBVA Partners) | No users, pilot or revenue | Main gap; one or two pilot production companies would unlock angels and accelerators |
| Team | Team quality, often a technical co-founder | Solo, non-technical founder building with AI | Significant weakness in angel and VC screens (GBA, Entertech); 20+ years of domain expertise is a differentiator for vertical SaaS; investors will probe execution and bus-factor risk |
| Language | Turkish programs dominant | Prefers Turkish | Not a barrier domestically; PİLOT's US track and foreign co-investors would need English |
| Sector | AI and SaaS most active | Vertical SaaS plus planned Claude AI layer | Favorable |

- The practical 12-month private sequence is:
  1. Join a BiGG implementing organization's Stage 1 program (İTÜ Çekirdek or another in the 2026-2028 plan). The implementing organization or its GSYF can co-invest.
  2. Run a Workup application in early 2027 (Turkish, online, no stated equity).
  3. Line up 1-2 pilot production companies.
  4. After BiGG and incorporation, pitch licensed angels, stressing the 100% deduction available because the project had TÜBİTAK support, and/or run a crowdfunding campaign. Expect single-digit millions of TL, and land a qualified investor first.
  5. Approach seed funds and GSYFs once there is paying usage.
- Media-sector players are better approached as pilot customers or strategic partners than as investors; no venture arms were found.

### Gaps
- No Turkish source states pre-seed requirements in aggregate (e.g., a survey of investor criteria). The synthesis is built from individual program and investor statements.
- No source quantifies how Turkish investors treat solo or non-technical founders, or AI-built products. The team-risk assessment is an inference.
