Private investors and EU/international programs are excluded (covered by other researchers). "Secondary" marks a non-official source (news site, mali müşavir, law or tax firm). ## 1. TÜBİTAK 1812 BiGG Yatırım: open 2027-1 accelerators, investment terms, shareholding rule, BİGG+ follow-on

### Takeaway
Two Stage 1 accelerators have confirmed open calls that feed the 2027-1 investment round: the İTÜ Çekirdek / BİGG DÜNYA consortium (ARI Teknokent + Düzce Teknopark; deadline 8 November 2026; training offered "yüz yüze/çevrimiçi") and Boğaziçi Teknopark's BiGG+ Venture Launchpad (İstanbul; window 8 October to 29 November 2026). The 2026-2 official call confirms 1,350,000 TL for 3% equity from the TÜBİTAK BiGG Fonu, an anonim şirket in which the entrepreneur is a founding partner, a follow-on of up to 1,350,000 TL (total stake capped at 5%), and a rule barring anyone who is a partner in any capital company **or sole proprietorship (gerçek kişi işletmesi)** as of the reference date. The 2027-1 Stage 2 investment window is planned for 25 January to 12 February 2027, and the company must be set up by 30 June 2027.

### Cited Findings

#### Program structure and the 2026-2028 cycle
- 1812 has three stages: Stage 1 = accelerator run by implementing organizations (uygulayıcı kuruluşlar); Stage 2 = pre-seed (tohum öncesi) investment by the TÜBİTAK BiGG fund for business plans awarded the "Mükemmeliyet Mührü"; Stage 3 = seed (tohum) investment (BİGG+) for companies that received pre-seed investment or completed a 1512 project — [TÜBİTAK 1812 program page](https://www.tubitak.gov.tr/tr/destekler/sanayi/ulusal-destek-programlari/1812-yatirim-tabanli-girisimcilik-destek-programi-bigg-yatirim)
- On 22 December 2025 TÜBİTAK announced 104 organizations in 35 supported projects as Stage 1 implementers for 2026-2028 (breakdown given as 40 TGBs, 38 universities/TTOs, 20 capital companies, 11 TEKMERs, 3 banks, 2 public research institutes; the page's own figures sum to 114, not 104) — [TÜBİTAK Stage 1 results announcement](https://tubitak.gov.tr/tr/duyuru/bigg-yatirim-asama-1-hizlandirma-programi-cagrisi-sonuclari-aciklandi)
- Stage 1 accelerator activities run 2 March 2026 to 31 July 2028. The investment plan schedules six Stage 2 calls, two per year in 2026, 2027 and 2028 — [1812 2026-2028 Yatırım Planı (Feb 2026)](https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2026-03/1812-2026-2028_Yatirim_Plani_Tasnif_Disi_260225.pdf)
- Planned 2027-1 Stage 2 calendar: applications to TÜBİTAK 25 Jan to 12 Feb 2027; document deadline 19 Feb 2027; evaluation 22 Feb to 22 Apr 2027; results 26 to 30 Apr 2027; company setup deadline 30 Jun 2027; support start 1 Jul 2027. Planned 2027-2: applications 26 Jul to 13 Aug 2027, results 1 to 5 Nov 2027, company by 31 Dec 2027 — [1812 2026-2028 Yatırım Planı](https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2026-03/1812-2026-2028_Yatirim_Plani_Tasnif_Disi_260225.pdf)
- Plan dates do shift. The plan listed 2026-2 applications as 31 Aug to 18 Sep 2026 with results 24 to 27 Nov 2026, but the v2 call text moved this to 31 Aug to 30 Sep 2026, with evaluation 12 Oct to 4 Dec and results 8 to 11 Dec 2026 — [1812-2026-2 Çağrı Duyurusu v2](https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2026-09/1812-2026-2_Cagri_Duyurusu_v2.pdf). The TÜBİTAK news page of 11 Sep 2026 still shows the older dates (evaluation 12 Oct to 20 Nov, results 24 to 27 Nov) — [TÜBİTAK 2026-2 announcement](https://tubitak.gov.tr/tr/duyuru/turkiyenin-ilk-yatirim-tabanli-girisimcilik-destek-programi-olan-bigg-yatirim-programinin-2026-2-cagrisi-acildi). An implementer calendar marks the later dates as "updated" — [BiGG Masters takvim](https://biggmasters.com/takvim)

#### Investment terms (2026-2 call, the latest official call text)
- The TÜBİTAK BİGG Fund invests 1,350,000 TL "kuruluşun %3 payı karşılığında". The maximum Stage 2 stake is 5% in total. A follow-on of up to 1,350,000 TL may be requested after the first-period report, if there is a triggering event (new investment round, binding customer orders, serial production), and the stake must stay at or below 5% after follow-on. Follow-on may be equity, convertible debt ("hisseye dönüştürülebilir borç") or both — [1812-2026-2 Çağrı Duyurusu v2](https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2026-09/1812-2026-2_Cagri_Duyurusu_v2.pdf)
- The implementing organization (or a legal entity acting for it) may co-invest up to 1,350,000 TL. Plans with committed co-investment skip the panel evaluation, and the amount is refunded if the seal is not granted — [1812-2026-2 Çağrı Duyurusu v2](https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2026-09/1812-2026-2_Cagri_Duyurusu_v2.pdf); [1812 Yatırım Planı](https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2026-03/1812-2026-2028_Yatirim_Plani_Tasnif_Disi_260225.pdf)
- Clean-tech add-on (UNIDO GCIP): for 3 selected clean-tech startups, an extra 900,000 TL for a further 2%, totalling 2,250,000 TL for 5%. This is not relevant to KAAPA, but it explains why some implementer pages quote "1.350.000 TL'den 2.250.000 TL'ye kadar" — [1812-2026-2 Çağrı Duyurusu v2](https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2026-09/1812-2026-2_Cagri_Duyurusu_v2.pdf); [KTÜ duyurusu, BİGG DÜNYA 2027/1](https://www.ktu.edu.tr/ofyazilim/duyuru/tubitak-1812-bigg-dunya-yatirim-destek-programi-2027-1donem-basvurulari-basladi)
- Implementation rules: Stage 2 fund stake "en fazla %5"; Stage 3 "en fazla %10". The board (Yönetim Kurulu) may set a lower or fixed rate, and the TL ceilings are announced in each call. The fund cannot buy existing founders' shares; it enters only through capital increases — [1812 içerik / önemli hususlar](https://tubitak.gov.tr/tr/destekler/sanayi/ulusal-destek-programlari/1812/icerik-onemli-hususlar); [1812 Uygulama Esasları (amended 07/02/2025, 72 YK)](https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2025-03/1812_Uygulama_Esaslari_72YK.pdf)
- Stage 2 project activities last at most 18 months including extensions. They cover concept design, technical/economic feasibility and technology development ("ticari prototip, demo, benzetim, yazılım algoritması vb."), with six-monthly monitoring — [1812 içerik / önemli hususlar](https://tubitak.gov.tr/tr/destekler/sanayi/ulusal-destek-programlari/1812/icerik-onemli-hususlar)
- Worked example of the capital mechanism (2025 amount): a company founded with 250,000 TL capital receiving 900,000 TL → 7,500 TL nominal capital increase + 892,500 TL share premium (i.e. 3% of 250,000 TL nominal) — [BiGG Yatırım SSS, 6.08.2025](https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2025-08/bigg_yatirim_cagrisi_sikca_sorulan_sorular_6.08.2025.pdf)
- The amount was 900,000 TL in 2025 and earlier 2026 material: Özyeğin's Girişim Fabrikası page (2025-2 call) and İTÜ Çekirdek's 6 March 2026 announcement both still say 900,000 TL — [Girişim Fabrikası BİGG](https://bigg.girisimfabrikasi.com/); [İTÜ Çekirdek, 06.03.2026](https://itucekirdek.com/?p=42212). BiGG Masters states the 2026 amount as 1,350,000 TL — [BiGG Masters takvim](https://biggmasters.com/takvim)

#### Company requirement and shareholding rule (exact wording)
- "Kuruluş" is defined as a "Türkiye'de yerleşik anonim şirket yapısındaki sermaye şirketi", and for Stage 2 the entrepreneur must be a "kurucu ortak". After agreement with the fund, the entrepreneur is asked to found a company matching this definition (Madde 16(3)), and registration on the call timeline is mandatory (Madde 16(4)) — [1812 Uygulama Esasları](https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2025-03/1812_Uygulama_Esaslari_72YK.pdf)
- The 2026-2 call specifies an anonim şirket "girişimcinin kurucu ortak olduğu", "Türkiye'de yerleşik". The call sets no capital amount, no minimum founder share and no age limit. Formation and contract signing for 2026-2 must be done by 31 Dec 2026 — [1812-2026-2 Çağrı Duyurusu v2](https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2026-09/1812-2026-2_Cagri_Duyurusu_v2.pdf)
- Partnership ban wording in the 2026-2 call and on TÜBİTAK's rules page: "sermaye şirketi veya gerçek kişi işletmesi herhangi bir işletmenin ortaklık yapısında yer alan kişiler başvuru yapamaz". Reference date: "hızlandırma programına kabul edildiği tarih itibarı ile". Shares in listed companies and shares acquired via crowdfunding platforms are exempt — [1812-2026-2 Çağrı Duyurusu v2](https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2026-09/1812-2026-2_Cagri_Duyurusu_v2.pdf); [1812 içerik / önemli hususlar](https://tubitak.gov.tr/tr/destekler/sanayi/ulusal-destek-programlari/1812/icerik-onemli-hususlar)
- The August 2025 FAQ gives a different reference date: "Ön başvuru tarihi itibarıyla sermaye şirketi veya gerçek kişi işletmesi...", where the ön başvuru date is when the implementing organization submits the idea to TÜBİTAK via eTEYDEB — [BiGG Yatırım SSS, 6.08.2025](https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2025-08/bigg_yatirim_cagrisi_sikca_sorulan_sorular_6.08.2025.pdf)
- The 2027-1 BİGG DÜNYA announcement repeats the acceptance-date version: "Programa kabul edildiği tarih itibariyle herhangi bir sermaye şirketi" (or sole proprietorship) — [KTÜ duyurusu](https://www.ktu.edu.tr/ofyazilim/duyuru/tubitak-1812-bigg-dunya-yatirim-destek-programi-2027-1donem-basvurulari-basladi). Boğaziçi lists anyone "in a capital company or sole proprietorship partnership structure" as ineligible — [Boğaziçi BiGG+](https://bogazicibigg.com/). Bilişim Vadisi phrases it as "no ownership stake in any business at the application date" — [Bilişim Vadisi BİGGVADİ](https://bilisimvadisi.com.tr/programlar/tubitak-bigg/)
- Also excluded: anyone who previously received the Ministry's Teknogirişim Sermayesi Desteği or TÜBİTAK 1512/1812 Stage 2 support — [1812 program page](https://www.tubitak.gov.tr/tr/destekler/sanayi/ulusal-destek-programlari/1812-yatirim-tabanli-girisimcilik-destek-programi-bigg-yatirim); [KTÜ duyurusu](https://www.ktu.edu.tr/ofyazilim/duyuru/tubitak-1812-bigg-dunya-yatirim-destek-programi-2027-1donem-basvurulari-basladi)
- Education condition: enrolled in or graduated from any ön lisans, lisans, yüksek lisans or doktora program. Turkish status is checked via PRODİS; foreign degrees need a YÖK denklik document. No age limit is stated — [1812-2026-2 Çağrı Duyurusu v2](https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2026-09/1812-2026-2_Cagri_Duyurusu_v2.pdf)
- Exclusivity: the shareholder agreement (YPSS clause 3.5) requires non-exempt entrepreneurs to work "yalnızca Şirket için" while they hold shares. Exempt shareholders must hold at most 20% ("pay sahipliği oranı %20'yi geçmemelidir"), not be the project leader, and hold no board or management role. The entrepreneur must leave their current employer "şirket kuruluş işlemleri başlamadan". IP created for the company's field belongs to the company. Agreements need wet-ink signatures, and originals are mailed to Türkiye Kalkınma Fonu — [BiGG Yatırım SSS, 6.08.2025](https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2025-08/bigg_yatirim_cagrisi_sikca_sorulan_sorular_6.08.2025.pdf)
- An implementer describes a 2-year exclusive-work commitment with a non-compete — [Bilişim Vadisi BİGGVADİ](https://bilisimvadisi.com.tr/programlar/tubitak-bigg/) (implementer page; the official YPSS was updated in October 2026: [YPSS kuruluş tek yatırımcı 2026_01_v2](https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2026-10/ypss_kurulus_tek_yatirimci_TBF_2026_01_v2.docx), contents not reviewed)

### Inferences
- **Fit: NOW, top priority.** Engin meets the education condition (lisans graduate), and there is no age limit. Keeping no company is correct, and the rule also covers a şahıs işletmesi (gerçek kişi işletmesi). He should not register any sole proprietorship (for example for freelance production invoicing) before acceptance and, to be safe given the FAQ wording, before the implementer's ön başvuru submission to TÜBİTAK.
- Realistic path: apply to İTÜ Çekirdek/BİGG DÜNYA by 8 Nov 2026 and/or Boğaziçi by 29 Nov 2026 → accelerator Nov 2026 to Jan 2027 → business plan to TÜBİTAK 25 Jan to 12 Feb 2027 → results late April 2027 → found the AŞ by 30 June 2027 (plan dates can slip, as 2026-2 did). If missed, the next round is 2027-2 (applications Jul to Aug 2027).
- Implied valuation: 1,350,000 TL for 3% equals a 45,000,000 TL post-money valuation (arithmetic on the official figures).
- The biggest practical constraint for KAAPA is exclusivity. YPSS 3.5 requires the founder to work only for the company and to leave any employer before company formation. If Engin still earns from production work, this must be planned before the AŞ is founded (around mid-2027).
- Solo founder: no rule forbids it, but "ekip yetkinliği" must score at least 7/10 at idea entry. A non-developer solo founder building with AI should present the domain depth (20+ years in production finance), the working demo and a credible plan for technical capacity (advisers, a planned hire, or a ≤20% non-managing shareholder who stays exempt from exclusivity).
- KAAPA's best thematic fit is "Haberleşme / Dijital Dönüşüm" (BİGG DÜNYA, Boğaziçi). None of the designated implementers is explicitly a creative-industries or SaaS track. ## 5. Technology development zones (teknopark/teknokent, Law 4691): 2026 status, 2024-2026 changes, pre-incubation

### Takeaway
The core 4691 incentives remain in force until 31/12/2028: income/corporate tax exemption on software, R&D and design income earned in the zone; a wage withholding-tax incentive and stamp duty exemption for R&D/design/support staff; a 50% SGK employer-share contribution; and a VAT exemption for listed software types produced in the zone. Two recent laws matter. Law 7524 (August 2024) introduced a 10% domestic minimum corporate tax from 2025, but the 4691 earnings exemption is deductible from its base, and new companies are exempt for their first three periods. Law 7555 (July 2025) capped the wage incentive at 40 times the gross minimum wage for payments from 1/8/2025. Incubation centers accept entrepreneurs who have not yet set up a business.

### Cited Findings
- **Duration:** "the incentives under Law No. 4691 remain in force until 31/12/2028" — [Yatırıma Destek özet, TGB girişimlere teşvikler (28/02/2021)](https://www.yatirimadestek.gov.tr/pdf/assets/upload/dosyalar/ozet_teknoloji_gelistirme_bolgelerindeki_girisimlere_yonelik_tesvikler.pdf). The wage withholding offset runs to 31/12/2028 — [GİB özelge, 10.02.2023](https://www.gib.gov.tr/node/168145/pdf). The 4691 temporary provision is quoted as "31/12/2028 tarihine kadar, bölgede çalışan AR-GE, tasarım ve destek personelinin..." — [Andersen, Law 7555 circular](https://tr.andersen.com/tr/images/pdf/and_37-arge-gt-duzenlemesi.pdf) (secondary). (The 2020 government summary still showed 31/12/2023 because it predates the extension — [Yatırıma Destek özet, TGB desteği (24/05/2020)](https://www.yatirimadestek.gov.tr/assets/upload/dosyalar/ozet-teknoloji-gelistirme_bolgeleri_destegi.pdf))
- **Corporate/income tax exemption:** earnings "solely from software, design, and R&D activities in the zone" are exempt — [Yatırıma Destek özet (2020)](https://www.yatirimadestek.gov.tr/assets/upload/dosyalar/ozet-teknoloji-gelistirme_bolgeleri_destegi.pdf). Restated in 2026: "31/12/2028 tarihine kadar gelir ve kurumlar vergisinden müstesnadır" (Geçici 2) — [Av. Dr. Fatih Aras, 07.06.2026](https://www.fatiharas.com/teknokent-vergi-istisnasi/) (secondary, law firm)
- **VAT:** deliveries of software produced exclusively in the zone are exempt for listed types: "sistem yönetimi, veri yönetimi, iş uygulamaları", sector software, internet, mobile and military command-control applications, for the same period as the income tax exemption (KDV Geçici 20). Input VAT is deductible — [Yatırıma Destek özet (2020)](https://www.yatirimadestek.gov.tr/assets/upload/dosyalar/ozet-teknoloji-gelistirme_bolgeleri_destegi.pdf); [Fatih Aras (secondary)](https://www.fatiharas.com/teknokent-vergi-istisnasi/)
- **SGK:** half of the employer premium share is paid from the budget, for staff whose wages fall under the income tax exemption — [Yatırıma Destek özet (2020)](https://www.yatirimadestek.gov.tr/assets/upload/dosyalar/ozet-teknoloji-gelistirme_bolgeleri_destegi.pdf)
- **Support staff cap:** support staff eligible for the withholding and SGK incentives may not exceed 10% of R&D and design staff, or 20% for firms with up to 15 employees — [GİB özelge (2023)](https://www.gib.gov.tr/node/168145/pdf); [Alomaliye on GVK Tebliğ 331](https://www.alomaliye.com/2025/09/04/gelir-vergisi-genel-tebligi-seri-no-331-gvk-331/)
- **Law 7555 change (2025):** the wage withholding incentive and stamp duty exemption apply only to "aylık brüt asgari ücretin kırk katını aşmayan kısmı". Wages for hours above 45 per week and overtime are excluded. Effective for payments from 1/8/2025; implemented by GVK Genel Tebliği Seri No 331 (RG 4.9.2025, no. 33007). At a 2025 gross minimum wage of 26,005.50 TL, the monthly cap was 1,040,220 TL — [Alomaliye, GVK 331](https://www.alomaliye.com/2025/09/04/gelir-vergisi-genel-tebligi-seri-no-331-gvk-331/); [Andersen, Law 7555](https://tr.andersen.com/tr/images/pdf/and_37-arge-gt-duzenlemesi.pdf) (secondary). Andersen gives the Resmî Gazete date as 24/8/2025, while the tebliğ cites the law as dated 20/7/2025 (the RG date conflict is unresolved)
- **Law 7524 (2024) minimum corporate tax:** 10% "yurt içi asgari kurumlar vergisi" for earnings from 2025. The "4691 sayılı Kanun kapsamındaki kazanç istisnası" and R&D/design deductions are deductible from the base. Newly operating companies are exempt for their first three accounting periods. A new GVK Art. 17 exemption covers shares that "teknogirişim şirketi" employers grant to employees, up to one year's gross salary (effective 2 Aug 2024) — [Andersen, Law 7524 circular](https://tr.andersen.com/tr/images/pdf/and_35-7524-sayili-kanun-duzenlemeleri.pdf) (secondary)
- **Venture-capital obligation (Ek 3, from 1/1/2022):** large exemption users must channel 3% into startups or VC funds. The TÜBİTAK BİGG+ fund is one eligible vehicle — [Fatih Aras (secondary)](https://www.fatiharas.com/teknokent-vergi-istisnasi/); [TÜBİTAK duyuru, 23.12.2024](https://tubitak.gov.tr/tr/duyuru/tubitak-bigg-girisim-sermayesi-yatirim-fonu-kuruldu)
- **Pre-incubation / incubation for founders without a company:** TGB startup incentives include discounted rent in an incubation center (kuluçka merkezi). Entrepreneurs with a project who have "henüz gelir ve kurumlar vergisine tabi bir işletme kurmamış" are covered; an "entrepreneur" is a real or legal person using the zone's services; applications go to the zone's managing company. Other listed incentives: wage support for basic-sciences graduates and tax incentives for venture-capital investments — [Yatırıma Destek özet (2021)](https://www.yatirimadestek.gov.tr/pdf/assets/upload/dosyalar/ozet_teknoloji_gelistirme_bolgelerindeki_girisimlere_yonelik_tesvikler.pdf). Applications are continuous via biltek.sanayi.gov.tr / teknopark.sanayi.gov.tr — [Yatırıma Destek özet (2020)](https://www.yatirimadestek.gov.tr/assets/upload/dosyalar/ozet-teknoloji-gelistirme_bolgeleri_destegi.pdf)
- BiGG implementers bundle incubation, for example BİGG DÜNYA's free shared office space at Düzce Teknopark — [biggdunya.com](https://biggdunya.com/)

### Inferences
- **Fit: NOW for incubation; AFTER FOUNDING for the tax incentives.** Joining a teknopark incubation or BiGG accelerator space as an individual, without registering a business, does not conflict with the BiGG partnership ban (inference from both rule texts).
- After the AŞ is founded and located in a TGB, KAAPA's SaaS revenue would plausibly qualify as "iş uygulamaları / sektörel yazılım" for the corporate tax and VAT exemptions until 31/12/2028. This depends on development being done in the zone, and remote-work limits apply.
- The 2028 sunset matters for planning. An exemption horizon of about 18 months (if founded mid-2027) is short unless the law is extended again. No extension beyond 2028 was found.
- The 40x cap does not bind at an early-stage salary level. The 45-hour/overtime exclusion and the support-staff ratio do matter for a tiny team (the 20% ratio applies to firms of 15 or fewer).
- The Law 7524 minimum-tax exemption for the first three periods plus the 4691 deduction mean no minimum-tax drag early on.

### Gaps
- Official, up-to-date 2026 Sanayi Bakanlığı text on remote-work percentages for TGB staff (secondary sources omit it; the Andersen note mentions a general 20% out-of-zone limit adjustable by the President).
- No extension beyond 31/12/2028 or 2026-dated amendment to 4691 was found. Absence is not proof of no change.
- Specific İstanbul, İzmir and Ankara teknopark pre-incubation programs open now (e.g. İTÜ Çekirdek, ODTÜ Teknokent, Teknopark İzmir, DEPARK) were not individually verified.
- The 2026 SGK employer-share support wording (the 2020 summary describes a five-year 50% support for R&D and support staff, which may be a separate provision).

## 6. Ministry of Trade services export supports (hizmet ihracatı destekleri) for software/IT and film/series

### Takeaway
A new Presidential Decision No. 10962 (RG 27/2/2026, effective 1/1/2026) replaced the old services-export, IT/E-Turquality, branding, technical-consultancy and logistics decisions. It funds software companies at 50% (up to +20 points for target countries) for digital product promotion abroad (up to 50M TL/year, 15M TL per product), hosting (5M TL/year), platform commissions, software licences, events, offices, IP registration and certification. It has no startup-specific clause and no revenue threshold for software firms; any Turkey-resident TTK company qualifies. It becomes relevant for KAAPA only after founding and once it markets internationally.

### Cited Findings
- **Decision:** "Hizmet İhracatının Tanımlanması, Sınıflandırılması ve Hizmet Sektörlerinin Desteklenmesi Hakkında Karar", No. 10962, dated 26/2/2026, RG 27/2/2026 no. 33181, effective 1/1/2026. Repeals 2014/10 (teknik müşavirlik), 2564 (markalaşma), 5447 (Türkiye Bilişim Sektörlerinin Uluslararasılaşması ve E-Turquality), 5448 (hizmet ihracatı) and 5449 (lojistik) — [Ticaret Bakanlığı, Karar PDF](https://ticaret.gov.tr/data/69a9d756269de18b843851b7/%C3%87er%C3%A7eve%20Karar.pdf)
- **Covered sectors (Hizmet Sektörleri Atılım Programı):** bilişim, kültürel ve kreatif endüstriler, finansal teknolojiler, dijital aracılık, eğitim, etc. Film is listed as a subsector in the Markalaşma Programı — [Alomaliye, 27.02.2026](https://www.alomaliye.com/2026/02/27/turkiyede-hizmet-ihracatina-yonelik-yeni-destek-programlari-ve-duzenlemeler/) (secondary, reproducing the decision)
- **Software-relevant supports (all 50% unless noted):**
  - dijital ürün tanıtım: up to 50M TL/year, max 10 products, 15M TL per product, 5 years
  - barındırma (hosting): 5M TL/year, 5 years
  - platform commissions (ICT): 20M TL/year, 4M TL per product
  - software licences: 2.5M TL/year
  - yurt dışı etkinlik katılımı: 1.5M TL per event, doubled for prestigious events
  - birim (overseas office): 6M TL per unit per year, max 25 units
  - tescil ve koruma: 2.5M TL/year
  - belgelendirme: 4M TL/year
  - reklam/tanıtım/pazarlama: 25M TL/year
  - Tekno Hızlandırıcı export projects: 25M TL per project, up to 2 years
  - UTPO: 35M TL per office per year, 5 years
  - Art. 44: up to +20 points for target-country activities
  - — [Ticaret Bakanlığı, Karar PDF](https://ticaret.gov.tr/data/69a9d756269de18b843851b7/%C3%87er%C3%A7eve%20Karar.pdf); annual caps may be revalued each calendar year — [Alomaliye](https://www.alomaliye.com/2026/02/27/turkiyede-hizmet-ihracatina-yonelik-yeni-destek-programlari-ve-duzenlemeler/)
- **Beneficiary definition:** Turkey-resident companies under TTK 6102, cooperatives, universities and others approved by the Ministry — [Alomaliye](https://www.alomaliye.com/2026/02/27/turkiyede-hizmet-ihracatina-yonelik-yeni-destek-programlari-ve-duzenlemeler/). The decision contains no startup- or young-company-specific provisions — [Karar PDF](https://ticaret.gov.tr/data/69a9d756269de18b843851b7/%C3%87er%C3%A7eve%20Karar.pdf)
- **Implementation circular (approved 5/3/2026):**
  - Software/IT beneficiaries (AI, cybersecurity, "diğer ticari yazılımlar", mobile apps, IT services) face no revenue-share threshold, unlike consulting/digital intermediation (51%).
  - Dijital ürün tanıtım (Art. 26) covers EK-14 promotional costs for software marketed abroad; claims are made after payment; one beneficiary per product.
  - Barındırma (Art. 25): if a Turkish version exists alongside the foreign-language version, support is calculated at 50%.
  - Software promotion is excluded from general advertising (Art. 21(8)) and handled under Art. 26.
  - Event participation needs pre-approval at least one month before (Art. 23).
  - Trademarks must be first registered in Türkiye in the beneficiary's name (Art. 22).
  - Annual financial information form from the second year after first payment (Art. 8).
  - — [Hizmet Sektörlerinin Desteklenmesine İlişkin Genelge](https://ticaret.gov.tr/data/69abe397269de14590bea074/0.%20Hizmet%20Sekt%C3%B6rlerinin%20Desteklenmesine%20%C4%B0li%C5%9Fkin%20Genelge.pdf)
- **Film/series:** "film" covers cinema, documentary, series, animation and program formats shot or produced wholly or partly in Türkiye. Dublaj ve alt yazı: 50%, up to 12M TL/year, only if the film is actually sold abroad, once per language. Film producers/distributors face no revenue threshold except for overseas-unit support (51% of revenue from film) — [Genelge](https://ticaret.gov.tr/data/69abe397269de14590bea074/0.%20Hizmet%20Sekt%C3%B6rlerinin%20Desteklenmesine%20%C4%B0li%C5%9Fkin%20Genelge.pdf); [Karar PDF](https://ticaret.gov.tr/data/69a9d756269de18b843851b7/%C3%87er%C3%A7eve%20Karar.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Fit: AFTER FOUNDING, once KAAPA has a foreign-language product and foreign sales or marketing.** Most useful lines: dijital ürün tanıtım (performance marketing abroad, for example toward co-productions and regional markets), barındırma (cloud hosting for foreign versions, 50% if a Turkish version also exists), software licences (2.5M TL/year cap; could cover third-party licences such as AI APIs if they fit the EK list; unverified), and trade-fair stands at MIPCOM/Cannes-type events if on the EK-17 list.
- The film-sector lines (dubbing, film branding) target content producers, not software vendors.
- Because claims are reimbursement-style (after payment) at 50%, the company must pre-finance the spending.

### Gaps
- Whether the beneficiary must be a member of an exporters' association (HİB) or register in a Ministry system before claiming; the circular excerpt was truncated before the general provisions.
- Detailed eligibility for "Tekno Hızlandırıcı İhracat Projesi" and UTPO (Arts. 35-38, 47 were not in the excerpt).
- Whether AI API or cloud costs qualify under "software licences" or "barındırma" (the EK lists were not read).

## 7. Ministry of Culture and Tourism cinema supports: any route for a production-technology/software project?

### Takeaway
No. The Sinema Sektörünün Desteklenmesi Hakkında Yönetmelik (RG 15.10.2019; last amended 18.06.2026) funds only film works and film activities: scripts, project development, production of every film type, foreign production, post-production, distribution, TV series, domestic screening, events and projects, cinema artists, and cinema equipment for public halls. No support type is dedicated to technology or software development. The closest non-production line, "Etkinlik ve proje" (festivals, archives, symposiums, sector training programs), is not designed for a software product.

### Cited Findings
- **Support types and articles:**
  - Senaryo ve diyalog yazım (Art. 17) and Proje geliştirme (Art. 13, producer)
  - Production: animasyon (18), belgesel (19), kısa film (20), ilk uzun metraj (14), uzun metraj (15), ortak yapım (16), yabancı film yapım (24)
  - Post-production: çekim sonrası (21), dağıtım ve tanıtım (22), dizi film (25), yerli film gösterim (23)
  - Etkinlik ve proje (26(1)), sinema sanatçısı (26(2)), sinema donanım for public cinemas of 100+ seats (26(3))
  - Applicants: "ülke içinde yerleşik gerçek veya tüzel kişiler" (Art. 9(1)). No support is dedicated to technology development; "yeni yöntem ve teknolojilerin kullanımı" appears only as an evaluation criterion for foreign productions (Art. 24(5)(ç))
  - — [Lexpera, current consolidated text](https://www.lexpera.com.tr/mevzuat/yonetmelikler/sinema-sektorunun-desteklenmesi-hakkinda-yonetmelik-1) (secondary legal database)
- **18 June 2026 amendment (RG 33284):** Art. 8 reorganized into pre-production, production and post-production groups. Dizi film changes: the "at least one season" requirement removed; first broadcast in Turkey within 3 years; per-episode and total caps set yearly by the Ministry. New re-application bar after enforcement under Law 6183. Nothing on technology, software or infrastructure — [Alomaliye, 18.06.2026](https://www.alomaliye.com/2026/06/18/sinema-destek-yonetmeligi-degisti-basvuru-ve-destek-kurallari-guncellendi/) (secondary, reproducing the RG text)
- **Original 2019 caps (2019 figures):** production supports up to 50% of budget; foreign film up to 30% of in-country spending accepted by the Ministry; screening up to 30% of ticket revenue — [NTV, 15.10.2019](https://www.ntv.com.tr/sanat/sinema-ve-dizi-sektorunu-guclendirecek-yonetmelik-resmi-gazetede,4J6-Xf9pd0Kh64f15TN0tQ)

### Inferences
- **Fit: NOT RELEVANT for building KAAPA.** At most, "Etkinlik ve proje" could co-fund a sector training program or symposium on production finance and budgeting, run by an eligible organizer. That would be an outreach and marketing activity, not product funding, and is untested.
- Indirect relevance: projects that receive Ministry production support must report budgets and costs. KAAPA's sealed budget versions and budget-versus-actual reports could be pitched as a compliance tool for supported producers (market insight, not a support line).

### Gaps
- The exact 2026 Ministry reporting formats for supported productions (which could inform product fit) were not researched.
- The official Sinema Genel Müdürlüğü announcement pages (sinema.ktb.gov.tr) were not fetched. The consolidated text comes from Lexpera.

## 8. Other public supports (İŞKUR, KGF, Türkiye Kalkınma Fonu, BiGG Fonu co-investment, state-linked corporate and municipal programs)

### Takeaway
Beyond BiGG, the remaining state-linked options are mostly later-stage or indirect. Türkiye Kalkınma Fonu (TKYB subsidiary) manages venture funds, including the TÜBİTAK BİGG fund and a "Teknoloji ve İnovasyon" fund that invests from seed to Series A. KGF planned about 7 new 2026 guarantee programs, including ones for young and women entrepreneurs, but no tech-startup product was confirmed. İŞKUR co-runs KOSGEB-linked entrepreneurship training. Türk Telekom's PİLOT and İBB's Tech İstanbul are state-linked corporate or municipal programs whose 2026 windows have closed. The genç girişimci tax exemption (GVK 20/A) applies only to sole proprietorships of under-29/30-year-olds, so it does not fit KAAPA.

### Cited Findings
- **BiGG Fonu co-investment:** the implementing organization (or an entity acting for it) may co-invest up to 1,350,000 TL alongside the BiGG Fund, which fast-tracks the plan past panel evaluation — [1812-2026-2 Çağrı Duyurusu v2](https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2026-09/1812-2026-2_Cagri_Duyurusu_v2.pdf). İTÜ ARI Teknokent's VC fund may co-invest in suitable İTÜ Çekirdek startups — [İTÜ Çekirdek](https://itucekirdek.com/?p=42212)
- **Türkiye Kalkınma Fonu:** founded 2019 as a subsidiary of Türkiye Kalkınma ve Yatırım Bankası; operates as a portfolio management company in PE, VC and funds-of-funds; manages seven funds (Bölgesel Kalkınma, TKYB Sermaye, Teknoloji ve İnovasyon, Yenilikçi ve İleri Teknolojiler GSYF, Kalkınma Katılım GSYF, TÜBİTAK BİGG, Invest 101); about 2.2 billion TL at end-2023. The "Teknoloji ve İnovasyon" fund is backed mainly by the Industry Ministry's Milli Teknoloji directorate and KOSGEB; TKF invests from seed to Series A — [Ekonomim/AA, 28.05.2024](https://www.ekonomim.com/ekonomi/stratejik-sektorlerdeki-sirketler-turkiye-kalkinma-fonunun-katkilariyla-buyuyor-haberi-745591) (secondary; 2023-2024 figures)
- **KGF (Kredi Garanti Fonu):** expects to launch about 7 new programs in 2026, including programs for young and women entrepreneurs with partner banks and external funds, ongoing KOSGEB projects, and a MEB-linked youth project. TOBB Nefes Kredisi (2025): about 60,000 firms, average loan about 1.5M TL; no 4th package planned at the time. KGF guaranteed about 250 billion TL of credit in 2025 — [Habertürk/AA, 27.12.2025](https://www.haberturk.com/kgf-2026-da-girisimcilere-odaklanacak-3848911-ekonomi) (secondary)
- **İŞKUR Girişimcilik Eğitim Programı (2016 description):** run since 2009 in cooperation with KOSGEB; for İŞKUR-registered persons aged 18 or over; 70 hours; İŞKUR covers participants' "zaruri gider" and work-accident and health insurance premiums — [Karar, 12.06.2016](https://www.karar.com/iskurdan-kendi-isini-kurmak-isteyene-girisimcilik-egitim-programi-155505) (secondary; 2016 information)
- **Türk Telekom PİLOT (TT Ventures):** 14th term applications opened 10 Feb 2026 and ran until May 2026; 12-week program, mentors, free office, Stanford-linked track; $3.2M cash and investment support to 131 startups to date — [Yeni Asır, 13.02.2026](https://www.yeniasir.com.tr/ekonomi/teknoloji/2026/02/13/kuresel-is-yapmak-isteyen-girisimler-pilot-destegi-aliyor) (secondary)
- **Tech İstanbul Growth (İBB brand):** for market-ready scaleups; offers PoC pilots with İBB units, workspace and networking, no cash; deadline 30 Sep 2026 (closed); focus on mobility, environment-energy and digital transformation — [Webrazzi, 16.09.2026](https://webrazzi.com/2026/09/16/tech-istanbul-growth-basvurulari-devam-ediyor/) (secondary)
- **GVK 20/A genç girişimci exemption:** only for first-time şahıs işletmesi owners aged 18 to under 29/30 (the source is inconsistent on the upper limit); A.Ş. and Ltd. are excluded; up to 400,000 TL of annual income exempt in 2026 for 3 years. The 12-month Bağ-Kur premium support was abolished for registrations from 1 Jan 2026 by Law 7566 (19 Dec 2025) — [IdeaSoft (secondary, updated 01.10.2026)](https://www.ideasoft.com.tr/kosgeb-ile-yeni-is-kurmak-icin-genc-girisimcilere-destekler/)
- **Teknogirişim employee-share exemption (Law 7524):** shares granted by "teknogirişim şirketi" employers to employees are income-tax-exempt up to one year's gross salary, reduced if sold within 12 years; effective 2 Aug 2024 — [Andersen, Law 7524](https://tr.andersen.com/tr/images/pdf/and_35-7524-sayili-kanun-duzenlemeleri.pdf) (secondary)

### Inferences
- **TKF funds: AFTER FOUNDING, after traction (seed/Series A).** Not a grant.
- **KGF: AFTER FOUNDING, with a bank loan.** Guarantees need a borrowing company and usually trading history.
- **İŞKUR/KOSGEB training: NOW (optional).** Useful only if a KOSGEB certificate is later required. Free KOSGEB online training exists anyway.
- **PİLOT and Tech İstanbul: watch 2027 windows.** Tech İstanbul needs a market-ready product and is city-focused, so it is a weak fit. PİLOT fits better (all of Turkey, tech-oriented).
- **GVK 20/A: NOT RELEVANT.** Wrong legal form, age likely over the limit, and it would break BiGG eligibility.
- **Teknogirişim share exemption: AFTER FOUNDING.** Useful for granting equity to a first technical hire or co-founder-employee.

### Gaps
- No official KGF product page for technology startups in 2026 was found.
- Current İŞKUR girişimcilik terms (allowance amount, whether a UGE certificate is still issued) were not found from an official 2026 source.
- Türk Telekom's state ownership share (via Türkiye Varlık Fonu) was not verified in this session, and neither were PİLOT's 2026 cash amount per startup or equity terms.
- No information on the TÜBİTAK BiGG Fonu's (Stage 2) fund size or its exact management structure (TKF vs. Kalkınma Girişim Sermayesi Portföy Yönetimi); sources mention both entities for different BiGG funds.
